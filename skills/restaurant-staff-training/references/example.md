# Example: a ten-minute front-of-house briefing

## Try this request

> Make a ten-minute French briefing for two new servers before dinner. Include
> three role-plays and answer notes. Use only these approved service facts.

Fictional facts approved by the owner on 19 November 2026:

- Dinner kitchen hours are 19:00–22:00; the venue stays open until 23:00.
- The fish dish is unavailable for this service.
- Servers look up bookings in the current booking system. A guest saying they
  made a reservation is not evidence that it appears there.
- Léa is the manager on duty. Servers escalate compensation requests to her.
  No compensation amount or refund policy has been supplied.
- For an allergy question, staff must check the current recipe and cross-contact
  information with the chef before answering; unresolved questions go to Léa.
- Mushroom risotto contains butter and Parmesan. No allergy-safe dish list is supplied.

## Timed session

| Time | Activity |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 minutes | Tonight's hours, unavailable dish, and who makes compensation decisions |
| 2–7 minutes | Three short paired role-plays, switching guest/server roles |
| 7–9 minutes | Debrief with the answer notes below |
| 9–10 minutes | Each starter states one check and one handoff they will use |

## Role-plays and observable answer notes

1. **“I booked for four; just seat us.”** Look up the booking and clarify the
   details needed to locate it. Do not manufacture a confirmation or promise
   a table before checking. If the lookup is unresolved, request the appropriate
   team decision without claiming it has already been made.
2. **“The risotto is vegetables, so it is fine for my milk allergy?”** Do not
   agree. The recipe includes butter and Parmesan. Explain that the chef must
   check the current recipe and cross-contact information before any suitable
   alternative is proposed. Do not claim that removing Parmesan makes it safe.
3. **“We waited too long; give us 50% off.”** Acknowledge the wait and involve
   Léa under the supplied procedure. Do not invent a discount entitlement or
   claim a refund was issued. Suggested phrase: “Je vais en parler à Léa,
   notre responsable de service.” Use it only when the server follows through.

## Readiness check

The facilitator observes whether each starter checks an unverified booking,
avoids the allergy assurance, and refers compensation to Léa. Until the session
has actually run, record readiness as **not yet observed**. The exercise is
service practice, not a food-safety certificate or an employee appraisal.
