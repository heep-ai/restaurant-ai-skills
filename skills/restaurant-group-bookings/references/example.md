# Example: a corporate dinner for 24

Fictional enquiry:

> Nous cherchons un dîner assis pour 24 personnes le 10 décembre 2026 à 20 h.
> Budget maximum : 60 € TTC par personne, boissons sans alcool comprises.
> Nous devons choisir vendredi 27 novembre. Avez-vous une salle privative ?

Owner-approved offer: private room physically seats up to 26; menu including
soft drinks costs €55 TTC per guest; setup fee €120 TTC. Existing terms require
a 30% advance payment on the full quoted total. Availability for 10 December
has not been checked. Cancellation conditions and dietary needs are not supplied.
No ingredient, staffing, or opportunity costs are known.

## Expected proposal arithmetic

| Item | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| 24 menus × €55 TTC | €1,320 |
| Setup fee, TTC | €120 |
| Total TTC | **€1,440** |
| Effective total per guest | **€60 TTC** |
| 30% advance payment if accepted under these terms | **€432** |
| Remaining balance | **€1,008** |

The offer fits the stated budget exactly. It is not €1,872: the advance payment
is part of the €1,440 total. Contribution/profitability cannot be calculated.

## Draft reply

> Bonjour, merci pour votre demande. Notre salle privative peut accueillir
> 24 personnes pour un dîner assis. Avec le menu et les boissons sans alcool
> à 55 € par personne, plus 120 € de mise en place, la proposition atteint
> 1 440 € TTC, soit votre budget de 60 € par personne.
>
> La disponibilité du 10 décembre à 20 h reste à confirmer. Pouvez-vous nous
> indiquer les éventuelles contraintes alimentaires du groupe ? Nous pourrons
> ainsi vérifier la date et finaliser une proposition complète.

Private owner notes: check calendar and dietary feasibility, supply the actual
cancellation terms, and plan a follow-up before the guest's 27 November decision.
No room is held and no deposit has been requested or collected.

## Variant: a minimum spend changes the answer

Suppose the owner now supplies a different approved offer: the same menu and
setup fee, plus a **€1,500 TTC food-and-drink minimum**. Any shortfall to the
minimum is charged; the €120 setup fee does not count toward it. This is a
separate scenario, not a retroactive condition on an accepted offer.

| Item | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Qualifying menus and soft drinks | €1,320 |
| Shortfall to the €1,500 minimum | €180 |
| Setup fee outside the minimum | €120 |
| Total TTC | **€1,620** |
| Effective total per guest | **€67.50** |
| Gap above the guest's total budget | **€180** |
| 30% advance payment | **€486** |
| Remaining balance | **€1,134** |

Do not quote €2,940 by adding the entire minimum to the original €1,440. Do not
say this variant fits the €60-per-person budget. Offer a cheaper configuration
only if the owner has actually supplied one; availability is still unchecked.

Pipeline entry: stage **qualified**, not confirmed; requested event 10 December;
guest decision 27 November; calendar check and terms completion are next actions.
The quote is not collected revenue and a draft follow-up is not a sent message.
