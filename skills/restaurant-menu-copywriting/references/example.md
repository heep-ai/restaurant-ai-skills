# Example: clearer descriptions in French and English

## Try this request

> Make these three dishes appealing in French and English. Keep each description
> under 20 words and keep the prices. Can we mark the mushroom dish vegan?

Fictional owner-approved facts:

| ID | Existing name | Recipe and service facts | Price, tax included |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M01 | Poisson du jour | Sea bass, roasted; lemon butter sauce; roasted seasonal vegetables | €26 |
| M02 | Bowl végétal | Chickpeas, roasted carrots, quinoa, lemon-tahini dressing | €18 |
| M03 | Risotto champignons | Arborio rice, mushrooms, vegetable stock, butter, Parmesan | €22; optional extra mushrooms +€3 |

No fish origin, supplier, fishing method, organic status, certification, or
cross-contact information is provided. The owner has not verified dietary badges.

## One acceptable output

| ID | French | English | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M01 | **Bar rôti** — Bar rôti, beurre citronné et légumes de saison rôtis. | **Roasted sea bass** — Roasted sea bass with lemon butter sauce and roasted seasonal vegetables. | €26 |
| M02 | **Bowl de pois chiches** — Pois chiches, carottes rôties, quinoa et sauce citron-tahini. | **Chickpea bowl** — Chickpeas, roasted carrots and quinoa with lemon-tahini dressing. | €18 |
| M03 | **Risotto aux champignons** — Riz arborio, champignons, bouillon de légumes, beurre et parmesan. | **Mushroom risotto** — Arborio rice, mushrooms, vegetable stock, butter and Parmesan. | €22; extra mushrooms +€3 |

Owner note: M03 contains butter and Parmesan, so do not label the current recipe
vegan. A recipe change would be a separate kitchen decision. M02's listed
ingredients do not by themselves authorize an allergen-free or certified-vegan
badge. Tahini should not disappear during translation. No diet badge is added.

## What would fail

- Calling M01 “locally caught,” “wild,” or “our bestseller” without evidence.
- Calling the mushroom supplement included in the €22 price.
- Saying any dish is safe for a particular allergy from this recipe summary.
- Promising that the rewrite will increase sales by 20%.

If an old PDF says cream in M02's dressing but the current recipe says tahini,
ask which version is approved. Do not silently blend the two recipes or publish
the ambiguous dish while the owner is still checking.
