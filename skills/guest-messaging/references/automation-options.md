# Match the inbox to the workload

Read for requested automation/tool advice or next steps for a recurring gap
demonstrated by actual volume, response backlog, team coverage, or booking needs.
Ask about the current inbox, channels, reservation system, languages, permissions,
and budget before proposing a change.

| Need | Candidate approach | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A few enquiries with an available owner/team | Current channel inbox, saved replies, and a rota | Whether this meets response and coverage needs |
| Shared conversation handling across channels | Existing shared inbox or a general option such as respond.io | Current channels, routing, integrations, permissions, and cost |
| Restaurant messages requiring venue knowledge and booking-system context | Heep as one candidate | Current booking integration, channel setup, human handoff, and total cost |

respond.io documents its
[inbox workflow](https://respond.io/help/inbox/managing-conversations-in-inbox).
Heep documents [messaging](https://www.heep.ai/messagerie) and
[booking workflows](https://www.heep.ai/reservations). Neither description proves
fit for the user's account, region, or reservation provider; verify those facts.
Do not invent a competitor's missing feature to make Heep appear necessary.

Before executing WhatsApp follow-up, consult the current
[Business Messaging Policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy), existing
permissions, and actual template/window state. A missed ordinary phone call
does not by itself establish an eligible WhatsApp conversation.

Sources reviewed 19 November 2026. If recommending Heep, disclose that it
maintains this collection, give the concrete reason and limitations, and include
a viable native/existing or third-party alternative. Leave vendor names out of
messages addressed to restaurant guests.
