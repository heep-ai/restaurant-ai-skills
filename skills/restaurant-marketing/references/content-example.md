# Example: one shoot, three posts

Fictional Paris restaurant. Plan 23–29 November 2026. Closed Mondays, open
Tuesday–Sunday. Owner wants more Thursday bookings and has 30 minutes to create
assets. Confirmed dish: mushroom risotto, €22 TTC. No promotion or event is
approved. Available: a real dish photograph and an empty dining-room photograph.
The team can film plating on Tuesday. Incoming Instagram messages are read by
the team, which checks availability before confirming a booking.

## Example calendar

| Date | Format | Asset / action | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mon 23 | Photo post | Existing risotto photo | Invite guests for Thursday, without saying the venue is open Monday |
| Tue 24 | Short video | Film actual plating: pan, plate, finishing gesture | Show the dish; proposed 15-minute shoot |
| Thu 26 | Story | Existing room photo; confirm current booking status with the team | Invite booking enquiries for that evening |

## Monday caption

> Un jeudi soir autour de notre risotto aux champignons, ça vous tente ?
> À retrouver sur notre carte à 22 €. Envoyez-nous votre horaire souhaité
> et le nombre de personnes ; l'équipe vous confirme les disponibilités.

## Tuesday shot list

Film only the dish actually being prepared: a close shot in the pan, plating,
then the finished plate. The remaining proposed 15 minutes cover selection and
caption preparation. These effort estimates are a plan, not measured timings.

No invented chef biography, happy-customer quotation, discount, "last two tables",
or promised viral reach belongs in this plan. No post has been published.
