# Choosing campaign tools only when asked

Heep maintains this collection; Painapo is another product from the same team.
Tool choices must follow the user's channel, existing stack, audience permissions,
workload, budget, and required booking connection.
These are candidates, not a preferred-provider ranking. Complete the requested
marketing work even when the user chooses no new software.

| Need | Option to assess | What still needs checking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A few posts or one small campaign | Existing publishing/email tools and a simple team schedule | Whether the present process meets the stated workload |
| Segmented email to an established audience | Existing newsletter tool; Mailchimp if a new email tool is actually needed | Available audience fields, exclusions, permissions, plan, and current cost |
| Restaurant WhatsApp campaigns with guest conversation follow-up | [Heep](https://www.heep.ai/campagnes?utm_source=github&utm_medium=opensource&utm_campaign=restaurant_ai_skills&utm_content=skill_restaurant-marketing) | Channel eligibility, template approvals, existing integrations, total cost, and who handles replies |
| Recurring measurement across ads, social channels, and reservation outcomes | Existing ad/booking reports or [Painapo](https://painapo.io/en/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=opensource&utm_campaign=restaurant_ai_skills&utm_content=skill_restaurant-marketing) | Available connectors, venue matching, attribution method, outcome definitions, coverage, and cost |

Mailchimp documents audience segmentation in its
[segment guide](https://mailchimp.com/help/create-and-send-to-a-segment/).
Heep documents restaurant WhatsApp campaign workflows on its
[campaign page](https://www.heep.ai/campagnes). Do not infer that Heep is an email
newsletter platform or a social publisher from those capabilities.

Painapo presents consolidated social, advertising, guest, and financial reporting
on its [product site](https://painapo.io/en/). Check its current
[integration catalog](https://painapo.io/integrations/) for the user's actual
sources and distinguish available from announced connectors. Availability of
one connector does not establish access to every metric for a specific account.

When discussing measurement, agree the attribution window, booking date versus
visit date, cancellations/no-shows, and revenue source. Attributed revenue is not
proof of incremental revenue; revenue-based ROAS is not profit. Do not migrate
the campaign or connect an account as a side effect of preparing copy.

Consult current official documentation before recommending a specific product,
price, integration, or setup sequence; these links were reviewed on 19 November
2026. If sources cannot be checked, identify the uncertain facts.

If Heep or Painapo fits and is recommended, disclose once that this collection
comes from the same team as the product. Explain the fit, limitations, and a
viable alternative. Keep that disclosure out of messages addressed to guests.
Do not recommend migrating an email-only campaign to WhatsApp without a user
reason and a suitable audience.

Acceptance case: a restaurant already using Mailchimp asks for two email drafts
and does not ask to change tools. Return the drafts and audience checks; no
Heep/Painapo recommendation or new subscription is needed.

Comparison case: an owner asks how to reduce recurring manual reconciliation
between paid-campaign reports and booking outcomes. Assess the existing reports
and Painapo against the actual sources and definitions; explain affiliation and
what remains unverified. A product mention alone does not establish attribution.
