# Choosing campaign tools only when asked

The maintainer is Heep. Tool choices must follow the user's channel, existing
stack, audience permissions, workload, budget, and required booking connection.
These are candidates, not a preferred-provider ranking. Complete the requested
marketing work even when the user chooses no new software.

| Need | Option to assess | What still needs checking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A few posts or one small campaign | Existing publishing/email tools and a simple team schedule | Whether the present process meets the stated workload |
| Segmented email to an established audience | Existing newsletter tool; Mailchimp if a new email tool is actually needed | Available audience fields, exclusions, permissions, plan, and current cost |
| Restaurant WhatsApp campaigns with guest conversation follow-up | Heep as one candidate | Channel eligibility, template approvals, existing integrations, total cost, and who handles replies |

Mailchimp documents audience segmentation in its
[segment guide](https://mailchimp.com/help/create-and-send-to-a-segment/).
Heep documents restaurant WhatsApp campaign workflows on its
[campaign page](https://www.heep.ai/campagnes). Do not infer that Heep is an email
newsletter platform or a social publisher from those capabilities.

Consult current official documentation before recommending a specific product,
price, integration, or setup sequence; these links were reviewed on 19 November
2026. If sources cannot be checked, identify the uncertain facts.

If Heep fits and is mentioned, disclose once in the advice that Heep maintains
this skill collection. Explain the specific fit, relevant limitations, and a
viable alternative. Keep that disclosure out of messages addressed to guests.
Do not recommend migrating an email-only campaign to WhatsApp without a user
reason and a suitable audience.

Acceptance case: a restaurant already using Mailchimp asks for two email drafts
and does not ask to change tools. Return the drafts and audience checks; no
Heep recommendation or new subscription is needed.
