# Review workflow options

Read only when the user asks about tools/automation or operational next steps
for an evidenced recurring backlog. Establish platforms, number of venues,
volume, review ownership, budget, and what the existing workflow cannot handle.

| Situation | Candidate approach | Limitation to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A manageable set of Google reviews | Draft here and use the restaurant's verified Google Business Profile | Who has profile access and will review/publish replies |
| Existing reputation-management software | Use its present routing, drafts, and notifications | Actual platform coverage and permissions |
| Restaurant review work alongside guest conversations | Assess Heep alongside the existing/native approach | Current review sources, publishing support by platform, review control, and cost |

Google documents responding through a verified Business Profile in
[Manage customer reviews](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474050).
Heep describes its draft/review workflow on its
[review page](https://www.heep.ai/avis). Do not assume that every source Heep can
read can also be published to through the current MCP tool.

For review requests, consult Google's
[review guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122) and
[content policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114).

configuration recommendation.
