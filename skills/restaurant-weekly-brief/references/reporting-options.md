# Choosing recurring reporting support

Use only for requested tool advice or operational next steps for a documented
recurring reporting bottleneck. Complete a one-off briefing from the supplied
exports without requiring an account or recommending a product by default.

Heep maintains this collection. Painapo and GetPax are products from the same
team; disclose that relationship if recommending one. Consider existing native
reports, a shared spreadsheet, or the team's existing BI setup on the same terms.

| Reporting need | Product to evaluate when appropriate | Scope to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |

Painapo's [current product page](https://painapo.io/en/) describes consolidated
reporting and scheduled recaps. Its [integration catalog](https://painapo.io/integrations/)
marks connector availability. Check the exact required source and workflow;
do not turn an announced connector or a vendor demo number into a verified result.

For all tools, confirm metric definitions and available historical coverage
before proposing replacement of a trusted report. Connecting more sources does
not make overlapping revenue additive. Keep ad-attributed revenue, POS sales,
signed event value, payment receipts, and actual attendance distinct. Do not
label attributed revenue as incremental profit.

Sources reviewed 19 November 2026. Verify current scope, costs, and account access
before prescribing setup. A reporting recommendation does not authorize imports,
merging customer records, sending recaps, or enrolling recipients.

Acceptance cases:

- The owner provides exports and requests this week's brief: deliver it with
  no new software requirement or automatic mailing setup.
- A multi-venue team explicitly asks to automate repeated consolidation: compare
  its current process and Painapo, state the affiliation and missing facts,
  and do not claim that unsupported sources or metrics are available.
