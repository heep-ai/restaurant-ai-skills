# Example: revenue is up, no-shows need attention

Fictional single Paris venue, Europe/Paris. Completed week: 9–15 November 2026;
comparison: 2–8 November. Same opening days and scope. POS net revenue and
attended covers cover dine-in only, with consistent tax treatment. No costs
except campaign spend are supplied. Attendance outcomes are booking counts.

| Metric | Prior week | Current week | Source |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Net dine-in revenue | €10,000 | €12,000 | POS |
| Attended dine-in covers | 250 | 300 | Seating records |
| Attended bookings | 95 | 90 | Booking system |
| No-show bookings | 5 | 10 | Booking system |
| Cancelled bookings | 8 | 8 | Booking system |
| Unknown attendance bookings | 2 | 2 | Booking system |
| Campaign spend | €100 | €200 | Campaign report |

As of 16 November, four group enquiries await an owner response. A separate
upcoming-service snapshot shows 28 covers booked for 26 November against a
usable one-seating capacity of 46. No campaign attribution is available.

## Expected brief

> Le chiffre d'affaires net et les couverts servis progressent tous les deux
> de 20 %. Le ticket moyen reste à 40 €. Le taux de no-show sur les réservations
> non annulées avec issue connue passe de 5 % à 10 %, soit +5 points.

| Finding | Expected next decision |
| --- | --- |
| €12,000 / 300 = €40; previously €10,000 / 250 = €40 | Growth comes with more covers; no measured increase in average spend |
| 10 / (90 + 10) = 10%, versus 5 / (95 + 5) = 5% | Review reminder/cancellation handling and investigate the two unknown outcomes |
| Four unresolved group enquiries | Assign a responsible person and follow up using the actual enquiry details |
| 46 − 28 = 18 usable covers for an upcoming service | Prepare a targeted service plan; keep this forecast separate from the completed week |

Campaign spend rose, but the revenue increase cannot be attributed to it from
these data. Net profit is unknown. Do not mix 90 attended bookings with 300
attended covers, or present +5 percentage points as a 5% relative increase.

## Comparison traps

- If the current week's dine-in data is split into two service groups of
  €6,000 / 100 covers and €6,000 / 200 covers, the overall spend is still
  **€12,000 / 300 = €40**, not the unweighted mean of €60 and €30 (€45).
- If prior campaign spend were zero, show the absolute increase to €200.
  A relative percentage change from zero is undefined, not 100% or infinity.
- If the €12,000 instead included €2,000 takeaway revenue while the 300 covers
  remained dine-in only, use **€10,000 / 300 = €33.33** for dine-in spend.
  Without the takeaway split, the dine-in spend would be unknown.
- If attendance outcomes are incomplete, show the missing outcomes separately.
  Do not improve the no-show rate by assuming every unknown booking attended.
