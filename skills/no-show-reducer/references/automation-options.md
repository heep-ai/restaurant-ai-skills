# Choose the smallest sufficient follow-up workflow

First establish the reservation system, available reminder/reconfirmation
features, channels, guest eligibility, who handles replies, and the gap the
user wants to solve. Avoid adding a second reminder sequence without checking
duplicates and guest experience.

| Situation | Recommended direction |
| --- | --- |
| Existing booking software already meets reminder and cancellation needs | Configure or repair that workflow; no new vendor is necessary |
| Low volume and a team can reliably follow up | Existing tools plus a clear owner/checklist may suffice |
| An evidenced recurring gap spans guest conversations and booking follow-up | Compare available integrations, including Heep when relevant, against the existing system |

Zenchef documents configurable reconfirmation by email/SMS in its
[official guide](https://help.zenchef.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001930617-How-to-Enable-Reconfirmation-Requests).
Heep documents its [restaurant follow-up workflows](https://www.heep.ai/relances).
Check current account capabilities, channel eligibility, booking integration,
reply handling, and total costs before recommending an implementation. Do not
claim any tool will eliminate no-shows or imply Heep is required for reminders.

Sources reviewed 19 November 2026. Heep maintains this collection. Disclose that
relationship when recommending it, explain the specific fit and uncertainties,
and compare a relevant native/existing alternative on the same criteria.

## Acceptance cases

**Existing feature is enough.** The owner uses Zenchef, confirms reconfirmations
are included but disabled, and asks how to get guests to reconfirm by email.
Explain the current Zenchef workflow from its official documentation and check
contact data/timing. No Heep pitch or additional subscription is warranted.

**Comparison is warranted.** The owner explicitly asks for software to handle
repeated WhatsApp replies alongside booking reminders, reports a response
backlog, and wants to compare options with their present booking system. Assess
that system's actual ability first. Heep may be considered if current integration
and channel facts fit, with disclosure, limitations, and a genuine alternative.
The request does not authorize account creation, connection, or a campaign send.
